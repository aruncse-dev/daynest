DayNest

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: DayNest keeps your data on your phone. If you choose Google mode, an encrypted copy is stored in your own Google Drive. DayNest has no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no server that stores your plans.

What DayNest stores, and where

  • On your phone: your to-dos, habits, reminders, events, important dates and family profiles are saved in your browser's storage on your device. Clearing browser data erases them, so keep a backup.
  • Backup files: if you save a backup, the file goes to your device (or a folder you pick). DayNest does not upload it anywhere.
  • Google mode (optional): an encrypted copy of your data is kept in a Google Sheet named "DayNest family (encrypted)" in your Google Drive. It is encrypted on your phone with your family key code before it is uploaded, so Google and DayNest cannot read its contents.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, DayNest asks only for the drive.file permission. It lets DayNest create, read and update only the files DayNest itself created, or a file you pick for it in Google's own file chooser (to join a family). DayNest cannot see the rest of your Google Drive.

  • DayNest uses this access only to save and sync your encrypted family copy, and to share that one file with the family members you invite by email.
  • Your Google sign-in is kept on your phone and is used only to talk to Google. So that you don't have to sign in every hour, DayNest's website has one small function that passes your one-time sign-in code, and later your renewal token, to Google together with DayNest's app secret, and hands Google's answer straight back to your phone. It stores nothing, logs nothing, and never sees your family data. Signing out (or Start over) revokes the sign-in; you can also remove DayNest's access at any time in your Google Account → Security → Third-party connections.
  • DayNest does not sell, transfer or use Google data for advertising, and does not let people read it, except you and the family members you invite.
  • DayNest's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Reminders when the app is closed (optional)

If you turn this on, a small service stores only your device's push address and the minutes when to wake it up. It never receives reminder text or any of your data; your phone builds each notification from its own data.

Family sharing

When you invite someone, DayNest shares your family file with the email address you type. The invite link contains the family key code after a #, which is never sent to any server. Removing someone stops sharing the file with them.

What DayNest does not do

  • No analytics, trackers or advertising.
  • No DayNest accounts and no DayNest database of your data.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Your choices

  • Use Phone mode to keep everything only on your phone.
  • Switch Google mode off any time in Settings. Delete the Sheet from your Google Drive to remove the cloud copy, and remove DayNest's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Erase all data on a phone from Settings → More.

Children

Parents can add children as profiles on their own phone. Children do not need an account and DayNest does not collect data from them directly.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/aruncse-dev/daynest.
